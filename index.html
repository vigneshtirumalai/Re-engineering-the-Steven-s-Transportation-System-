      GalliMaps compares alternative paths and picks the one that stays off Bangalore's arterials — Outer Ring Road, Hosur Road, Old Airport Road and friends — as long as it isn't wildly slower.

      The idea: Google optimizes for ETA, which usually means the big, jammed roads. GalliMaps does the opposite — it scores each candidate route by how much of it runs on major arterials and highways, then prefers the one with the least "main-road exposure" within your time budget.

      How a road is judged "main": named Bangalore arterials & ring roads, highway references (NH/SH), and long fast-moving segments (a proxy for big roads).

      Honest limits: this rides on free public routing (OSRM) and its handful of alternative routes — it can't invent a gali the router never proposes. For "true" inner-road routing you'd run a custom Valhalla/GraphHopper profile; see the README for that upgrade path.
